Delete Your Cravitoo Account & Data

Applies to the Cravitoo employee app and the Cravitoo Partner vendor app.

You can delete your Cravitoo account and associated data at any time. This page explains the two ways to do that, what gets removed, and what we are legally required to keep.

Option 1 — Delete from inside the app (recommended)

The fastest way. Deletion starts immediately and completes within a few minutes.

  1. Open the Cravitoo (or Cravitoo Partner) app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab (bottom-right).
  3. Scroll to Privacy & Data and tap Delete My Data.
  4. Read the confirmation dialog, type DELETE to confirm, then tap Delete Permanently.
  5. You will be signed out and your account will be removed. You’ll receive a confirmation email within 24 hours.

Option 2 — Request deletion by email

If you can’t sign in, or you prefer a manual process, email us:

  • To: support@cravitoo.com
  • Subject: Delete my Cravitoo account
  • Body: Include the email address or phone number registered on the account so we can verify your identity.

We respond within 30 days, as required under India’s Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 (DPDP Act). In most cases the account is deleted within 3 business days of verification.

What we delete

When your deletion request completes, the following are permanently erased from Cravitoo systems and any active backups within 90 days:

  • Your account profile — name, email, phone, dietary preferences, notification settings.
  • Your saved addresses, saved cards references (we never store full card numbers; only Razorpay tokens, which are also revoked).
  • Your order history excluding invoices required by tax law (see below).
  • Your feedback, ratings and reviews submitted through the app.
  • Your device push-notification tokens, so we stop sending you any further notifications.
  • Your cart, favorites, and loyalty points balance.
  • All authentication sessions and one-time password (OTP) records.
  • For vendor accounts on Cravitoo Partner: your menu items, vendor profile, if you are the sole owner of the vendor.

What we are required to keep, and for how long

Indian tax and consumer-protection law obliges us to retain a limited set of transactional records even after account deletion. These are stored in a locked, access-restricted archive and are not used for marketing or profiling.

DataRetentionLegal basis
GST tax invoices for orders you paid for 7 years from the end of the financial year Rule 56, CGST Rules, 2017
Payment settlement records (order id, amount, timestamp) tied to those invoices 7 years Companies Act, 2013 § 128(5); Income-Tax Act, 1961 § 44AA
Refund and dispute records 7 years Consumer Protection Act, 2019
Anonymised, aggregated analytics (no personal identifiers) Indefinitely Not personal data under DPDP

After account deletion, your name, email, phone and other identifiers are stripped from these records; only order-level financial data remains for audit and tax purposes.

Reactivating a deleted account

Once deletion is complete, we cannot recover the account. If you want to use Cravitoo again, sign up fresh from the app.

2. Scope of this Policy

This policy applies to Personal Data we collect when you download, install or use the Cravitoo or Cravitoo Partner mobile app; sign in via OTP or email; browse cafeterias, vendors and menus; place, pay for, or pick up food orders; receive push or email notifications from us; contact us for support; or use the Cravitoo Partner app to accept and fulfil orders as a vendor.

It does not cover data practices of your employer, your cafeteria vendor, or third-party services we integrate with — each has its own privacy policy.

We do not collect precise or coarse location, contacts, SMS, call logs, media library contents, biometric data, advertising identifiers (IDFA / GAID), or sensitive personal attributes such as health, sexual orientation, caste, religion or political data. We do not store camera frames or photos.

3.4 Camera (Cravitoo Partner app only)

The vendor app uses your device camera only to scan customer pickup QR codes. The decoded QR token is sent to our backend to mark an order as "picked up". No photos, video frames or camera streams are transmitted or stored by us.

3.5 Children's data

The Services are intended for adult employees at partner corporate sites. We do not knowingly collect Personal Data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has provided us data, email info@cravitoo.com and we will delete it.

8. Your rights

  • Right of access — request a copy of the Personal Data we hold about you.
  • Right to correction and completion — ask us to correct inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Right to erasure — ask us to delete your Personal Data (subject to legal retention obligations).
  • Right to withdraw consent — with future effect, at any time, without penalty.
  • Right to grievance redressal — see Section 12 below.
  • Right to nominate — under s. 14 of the DPDP Act, nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of your death or incapacity.

In-app data rights already shipped: Profile → Download my data returns a JSON export of your account, orders, feedback and notification preferences. Profile → Delete my account runs a 10-second countdown + type-DELETE confirmation and then deletes your account and Personal Data (subject to Section 7 retention). You can also exercise any right by emailing info@cravitoo.com.

9. Security

We apply reasonable security safeguards, including TLS 1.2+ in transit, encryption at rest for the MongoDB Atlas cluster, hashed OTPs, short-lived JWT access tokens and rotating refresh tokens, SecureStore (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore) for tokens on device, role-based access control for internal staff, and regular dependency and security reviews. If we become aware of a breach that is likely to result in significant harm, we will notify the Data Protection Board of India and affected users as required by the DPDP Act.

10. Cookies & similar technologies

The mobile apps do not use browser cookies. On cravitoo.com we use only strictly necessary cookies; if any analytics or preference cookies are introduced in the future, we will update this policy and, where required, obtain your consent.
